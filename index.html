About the workshop

Seven days at the frontier of light and quantum communication

The Photonics Spectrum 2026 is a seven-day workshop focused on emerging developments in optical interactions, integrated photonics, materials, devices, and quantum communication.

The programme brings together technical lectures, hands-on training, industry-oriented learning, professional development sessions, and opportunities for interaction between academia and industry — featuring experts who bring participants exposure to contemporary research, technological developments, practical skills, and career-oriented perspectives.

Conducted entirely online, each day runs for approximately five hours: three hours of technical lectures and two hours of hands-on activity.

About Optica

Optica (formerly OSA), Advancing Optics and Photonics Worldwide, is the society dedicated to promoting the generation, application, archiving, and dissemination of knowledge. Founded in 1916, Optica's publications, meetings, and online resources fuel discoveries and accelerate scientific, technical, and educational achievement.

About IETE

The Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers (IETE), established in 1953, is India's leading professional society for the advancement of Electronics, Telecom, IT, and Computer Science — with over 1,25,000 members and 63 centres across India and abroad. IETE is recognised by the Government of India as a Scientific and Industrial Research Organization (SIRO) and an Educational Institution of National Eminence.
